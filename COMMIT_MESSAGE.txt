Engine: AU-2027-28 regime (negative gearing, CGT, SMSF LRBA, APRA DTI)

Adds the post-2026-budget tax regime as a date-selected layer beside AU-2026-27.
The structural changes bite from their real start dates; today's modelling is
unchanged until the engine is actually asked about a post-budget deal or a
future financial year.

Tax data (packages/engine/src/tax-data.ts)
- New TAX_DATA_2027_28 block (14% bottom bracket from 1 Jul 2027; other federal
  levers carried over; state duty/land-tax scales inherited unchanged).
- taxDataFor(date) selector + fyStartYear(date) helper. Current in-force block
  stays AU-2026-27 until 1 Jul 2027 — nothing changes for today's dates.
- TaxData gains optional negativeGearing + cgtRegime descriptors.

Regime rules (packages/engine/src/regime.ts, new) — pure, testable decisions
- negativeGearingRestricted(): established dwellings acquired after budget night
  (12 May 2026) have losses quarantined from FY2027-28; new builds and
  pre-budget (grandfathered) acquisitions keep the offset.
- smsfResidentialLeverageAllowed(): new residential LRBAs banned from 10 Aug
  2026; business real property and existing LRBAs unaffected.
- dtiAssessment(): flags DTI >= 6 against the APRA cap (<=20% of new lending).

CGT (packages/engine/src/cgt.ts)
- New opt-in "indexed-floor" regime: cost-base indexation + a 30% minimum
  effective rate on the real gain (established), the new-property election
  (lower of discount vs indexed), and a time-apportioned pre/post 1 Jul 2027
  straddle split. SMSFs keep the 1/3 discount; company unchanged.
- Default path ("discount") is byte-for-byte the prior behaviour, so existing
  CGT checks are untouched.

Wiring (packages/engine/src/engine.ts)
- project(): date-aware negative-gearing quarantine in the loss branch, keyed
  off the property's purchase date + new/established flag and the modelled FY.
  Records without a post-budget purchase date are unaffected (seeds included).
- serviceability(): DTI >= 6 flag (dtiHigh/dtiNote).
- verdictOf(): flags for quarantined negative gearing, the SMSF residential
  LRBA ban, and DTI >= 6.

Assurance (packages/engine/src/assurance/checks.ts)
- +19 known-answer checks (40 -> 59): 2027-28 income tax, the date selector and
  FY helper, the negative-gearing quarantine decisions, indexed CGT + the 30%
  floor + the new-property election, the SMSF LRBA ban, and the DTI flag.

Verified: engine 59/59; both apps tsc clean; both production builds green. Seed
projection outputs unchanged (grandfathered), confirming the change is inert on
legacy records.
