so existing records and the seed model are unchanged unless the new fields/flags
are set.

tax-data.ts
- lowIncomeTaxOffset() (LITO, up to $700, non-refundable, nil above ~$66,667),
  applied in both the 2026-27 and 2027-28 income-tax scales. Nil at the existing
  check incomes, so those figures are unchanged.
- registrationFees per state + registrationFeesTotal() — small fixed title
  transfer + mortgage registration charges (indicative; verify per LRS).

engine.ts
- borrowingCostAmortisation() — s25-25 borrowing costs deducted over 5 years (or
  the loan term if shorter); driven by a new loan.borrowingCosts field.
- acquisition(): opt-in auto registration fees (entry.autoRegistrationFees) added
  to cash-in + cost base; off by default to avoid double-counting a manual line.
- project(): borrowing-cost deduction in the tax calc; vacant-land holding-cost
  denial (s26-102) via tax.vacantLand — when set, holding-cost deductions are
  denied until a usable structure exists and is rented.
