# Terravado — future partnership ideas (parked)

_Not on the critical path. Revisit once the core product is built and validated._

## Property-management referral network (via a friend's agency software)
A friend works behind the scenes with real estate agencies across Australia — auditing,
rent-roll valuations and property-management due diligence — and is building software embedded
in those agencies.

**The idea:** integrate Terravado with her software and an **ethical, audit-vetted property-
management referral network**. Investors who buy through a Terravado-backed adviser need property
management next; refer them to agencies in the network, with Terravado participating as part of an
exclusive, vetted network and feeding leads to the businesses her software is in.

**Why it's strong**
- Distribution into agencies already running her software — a warm channel into the exact
  businesses Terravado would otherwise take years to reach.
- A genuine investor-lifecycle adjacency with **recurring** revenue, not one-off.
- A rent-roll data angle: real rental and vacancy data to feed Terravado's rental modelling,
  with the right consent.

**The ethical edge (what lifts it above a lead-sale):** because her work is independent audit and
due diligence, the network can refer only to managers who pass vetting — "we only refer you to
property managers who clear an independent audit." A real, defensible differentiator.

**Status:** increment-N partnership, after the core is built and validated. Rests on an existing
personal relationship — approach that on its own terms, separate from the business, when the
timing's right.
